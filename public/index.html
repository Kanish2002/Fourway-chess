Teams

Your partner sits opposite. Checkmate either enemy king to win. You cannot capture your partner’s pieces.

Board controls

Click or drag to move. Right-drag to draw an arrow; right-click marks a square. Shift-drag also draws arrows. Left-click clears annotations.
